How each report was placed

  1. One report, one count. The export pages overlap, so some reports come twice. Each id counts once.
  2. By tenant, not by intake tag. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant’s locale and booking form (causes.ts):
    • a report through the form of a booking-v3 tenant is hint: the form tells people to type month first, then reads the date day first;
    • every other report from a day-first tenant is parser.
  3. One-day moves are their own problem. The desk already reads some reports as one-day moves (desk-notes.json). They are not month flips: a month-first parser cannot turn 12 May into 11 May.
